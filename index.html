How This Strategy Works

Key Strategy Questions Answered

Plain-English explanations backed by the 16-year NSE archive audit and 21,600-configuration grid tests.

1. Why use 4 market health signals instead of a simple -15% drop rule? ▾
Testing 21,600 single-threshold crash rules showed that any single drawdown number overfits past crash depths (out-of-sample rank correlation was near zero: Spearman ρ = -0.091). Replacing it with 4 causally normalized orthogonal blocks — Trend (44%), Breadth (34%), Macro/Gold (12%), and Volatility/VIX (10%) — converged to identical weights across periods and lifted 16-year return from 15.79% (R2) to 18.16% (R3) while winning every blind test window.
2. When does the strategy move back into stocks after a crash? ▾
Simple on/off switches cause excessive whipsaw churn (~501% annual turnover). The 4-block model steps down fast during sell-offs (8-day smoothing) and climbs back steadily (22-day smoothing with a 25-day wait, requiring composite > +0.18 to return to 100% equity). An explicit Zweig Breadth Thrust override (21-day breadth surge ≥ +25 pts and > 50%) lets it catch sharp V-bottom recoveries like 2020 COVID and March 2023 cleanly.
3. Why switch between Gold and Cash instead of holding 50/50? ▾
Liquid cash funds face a 30% debt-slab tax (Sec 50AA), whereas Gold held over 1 year is taxed at 12.5%. Higher Gold beat static 50/50 historically — except static 100% Gold suffered a -31.7% slump in 2026. The causal TrendGold150 gate holds GOLDBEES when Gold trades > 2% above its 150-day EMA and automatically shifts to LIQUIDCASE when Gold falls > 2% below it, saving +16.8% during the 2026 slump.
4. Why does "50% Top-20 + 50% Top-30" hold 30 stocks instead of 50? ▾
Because the Top 20 stocks are already inside the Top 30. Every Top-20 leader receives weight from both sleeves (2.50% + 1.67% = 4.17%), while Ranks 21–30 appear once (1.67%). You hold exactly 30 unique stocks with a 2.5× tilt toward the strongest leaders. Under ₹3.5L, Sequential-SlotCap skips any single stock priced above its rupee slot and sweeps leftover rupees into LIQUIDCASE.
5. Why not hedge with Index Futures, Put Options, Gilts, or Low-Vol stocks? ▾
16-year head-to-head tests show: internal Low-Vol stocks still crash hard (-40.96% DD, 0.93 stress correlation); monthly 5% OTM Put options bleed VIX-scaled theta (0.47 Sharpe); Long Gilts stay capped around 0.48 Sharpe with 30% debt tax; and Short Index Futures (0.81 Sharpe) require ₹25L–₹1Cr+ due to ₹16.5L NSE lot minimums and 30% F&O tax. TrendGold150 works cleanly from ₹1L upward.
6. How do we know the 16-year backtest has no data leaks or survivorship bias? ▾
All 419 NSE ETFs were purged from equity rankings, historical midcap constituents were rebuilt semi-annually from official NSE archives (772 liquid midcaps, 2010–2026) with a 1-day lagged mask to remove forward look-ahead, and every finalist was re-verified on an exact T+1 Python FIFO tax-lot simulator deducting 0.15% one-way friction and Indian capital-gains taxes.
